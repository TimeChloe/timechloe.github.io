Hi, I'm Yue Zhang, a Ph.D. candidate in the [Cyber-Physical Systems Group](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/cps/) at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/), supervised by [Prof. Matthias Althoff](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/cps/members/prof-dr-ing-matthias-althoff/). I joined the group in March 2026.

I received my M.Sc. in Mechatronics and Robotics from TUM in December 2025. I wrote my master's thesis at the [Chair of Robotics, Artificial Intelligence and Real-Time Systems (AIR)](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/air/) under the guidance of [Kejia Chen](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/air/people/kejia-chen-msc/), supervised by [Prof. Alois Knoll](https://www.professoren.tum.de/knoll-alois-christian) and [Prof. Sami Haddadin](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/sami-haddadin/). Before that, I did my semester thesis and worked as a student research assistant at [KI.Fabrik](https://kifabrik.mirmi.tum.de/) ([MIRMI](https://www.mirmi.tum.de/)) on multi-modal demonstrations, teleoperation, and cable manipulation. I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in 2020.

My research focuses on **safe physical human-robot interaction**, with interests in energy-aware control, compliant control, and robotic manipulation.

I supervise master's and semester theses. If you are interested in these topics, please email me a short introduction with your transcript and CV. Current topics are listed on my [group page](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/cps/members/yue-zhang-msc/).
